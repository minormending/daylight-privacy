Daylight privacy policy

For the Daylight Android app, version 1.0.0. Last updated: October 29, 2026.

Daylight shows the sun and daylight where you are. It works offline. There is no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. The app does not make network requests or send your information to us or to a server.

Your place

You can choose a city without giving location permission. If you choose to use your location, Daylight asks once for approximate location while you are using the app and takes one location reading. It does not track you in the background.

The reading is used only on your device to calculate sun times and find a nearby city name in a city list included with the app. If no city is nearby, the name is “Your location.” Before saving a device location, Daylight rounds latitude and longitude to 0.01 degrees. It also saves a time zone so the displayed times follow the chosen place.

What stays on your device

Daylight saves your chosen place, whether location has already been requested, clock format, notification preference, and a record used to avoid duplicate reminders. These stay in the app's local storage. The chosen place and clock format are also made available to Daylight's own home-screen widgets on the same device, so they can display the light without opening the app. Daylight does not send the saved coordinates to other apps or services. When you add a widget, your home-screen launcher displays its rendered sky, place name and daylight information. The Android release disables app cloud backup and excludes this data from device transfer.

Optional reminders

Light nudges are off until you turn them on. If enabled and you grant notification permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device, at most one per local day. It does not register for remote push notifications. You can turn nudges off in the app, use a reminder's “Turn off” action, or disable notifications in Android settings.

Your choices and deletion

You can pick a different city at any time and revoke location permission in Android settings. To delete the app's saved information, clear Daylight's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Uninstalling also removes its widgets and scheduled reminders. There is no online account or server copy to delete.
